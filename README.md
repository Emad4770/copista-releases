# Copista

**A faster, friendlier clipboard history for Windows.** Press **Win+V**, pick what
you copied earlier, and it's pasted right where you were typing.

**[⬇ Download the latest version](https://github.com/Emad4770/copista-releases/releases/latest)**
· Windows 10 (2004 or newer) and Windows 11 · Free

---

## Why Copista

- **Opens on Win+V.** Copista takes over the Windows clipboard shortcut, so there's
  nothing new to learn. Prefer the Windows one? Turn it off in Settings.
- **Pastes where you were typing.** The picker never steals focus from your app.
  Click an item (or press Enter) and it lands in place.
- **Finds things fast.** Search as you type and filter by **Text**, **Images**,
  **Files** or **Links**. Every item shows the app it came from and when.
- **Keeps what matters.** Pin the things you use all the time. Pins stay until you
  unpin them.
- **Snippets for text you retype.** Addresses, replies, signatures: save them once and
  paste them with **Win+Alt+V**. Snippets can fill in today's date, the time, or what
  you last copied.
- **Private by design.** Your history stays on your PC, encrypted to your Windows
  account. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Light, dark, and out of your way.** Follows your Windows theme. It lives in the
  tray and opens wherever suits you: a corner, next to the mouse, or where you're
  typing.

## Privacy

- **Stays on this PC.** Everything you copy is saved encrypted on this PC, never in
  the cloud.
- **Password managers respected.** Copies an app marks as private (like most password
  managers) are never saved.
- **Ignored apps.** List apps whose copies are never saved. It starts with common
  password managers, and you can add any app.
- **Hide an item.** Hide anything sensitive: it shows as "Hidden", search won't find
  it, and you can still paste it.
- **No account.** Copista doesn't need an account and doesn't track you.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Win+V** | Open your clipboard history |
| **Win+Alt+V** | Open your snippets |
| **↑ / ↓**, **Enter** | Choose and paste |
| **Shift+Enter** | Paste as plain text (no fonts or colors) |
| **Ctrl+Enter** | Copy without pasting |
| **Ctrl+1 … Ctrl+9** | Paste the 1st … 9th item |
| **Ctrl+P** / **Ctrl+H** | Pin / hide the selected item |
| **Delete** | Delete the selected item |
| **Tab** | Switch between Clipboard and Snippets |
| **Esc** | Clear the search, then close |

You can change both hotkeys in Settings.

## Install

1. Download **Copista-Setup-&lt;version&gt;.exe** from the
   [latest release](https://github.com/Emad4770/copista-releases/releases/latest).
2. Run it. It installs just for you, with no admin rights needed.
3. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**.
   The installer isn't code-signed yet; that's why Windows asks.
4. Copista needs the free **.NET 10 Desktop Runtime**. If it's missing, the installer
   offers to download it.

Copista starts in the tray. Press **Win+V** to begin. To have it start with Windows,
turn on *Launch at startup* in Settings.

**Updates** install themselves: Copista checks once a day and offers the new version.
You can also check in Settings → Updates.

**Uninstall** from Windows Settings → Apps. Your history and settings stay in
`%LocalAppData%\Copista` in case you come back; delete that folder to remove them.

## Good to know

- Copista keeps its own history, so it works even with Windows clipboard history
  turned off.
- In apps running as administrator, Win+V opens the Windows clipboard instead
  (Windows doesn't let normal apps act inside admin windows).
- Text keeps its formatting when pasted. Use **Shift+Enter** when you want plain text.
